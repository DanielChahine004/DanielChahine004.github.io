The UTS Biomedical Engineering Society runs hands-on engineering projects that give students the practical, cross-disciplinary experience that is hard to get in class. After our computer-vision-controlled bionic hand in 2024, where I was software lead, we set out in 2025 to build a humanoid robot. As Chief Technical Officer from 2025 to 2026, I lead a team of about 20 volunteers from biomedical, electrical, mechanical, mechatronic and software engineering.

**We won the 2026 ActivateUTS Award for Most Innovative Student Initiative.**

## Phase 1 — humanoid torso (2025)

We built a humanoid torso: a 3D-printed head with sensors and servo-driven arms, mounted on an aluminium-extrusion frame that houses the power and control electronics.

- Architected the system integration: the interfaces between components, high-bandwidth serial communication, and how software is split between the embedded controllers and the host computer.
- Managed the team and a $2,000 budget across the society's two projects, the bionic hand and the humanoid.
- Showcased the robot at UTS TechFest 2025 and at the society's industry networking nights, reaching more than 500 attendees across five events.

## Phase 2 — mobility and on-board AI (2026)

The next phase turns the static torso into an untethered, mobile platform:

- An NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano replaces the laptop and ESP32 as the main computer, for real-time computer vision and a locally run language model.
- A Teensy 4.1 microcontroller handles high-speed servo control and sensor integration.
- A motorised wheelbase and a 12 V battery system let the robot move and run without mains power.

We secured a $1,000 AUD ActivateUTS Strategic Project Grant to fund this phase.

## Growing the team

- Secured industry support for the project, including a 3D printer donated by QIDI Technology in 2025, which the team now uses for structural parts.
- Planned a free six-part workshop series (Fusion 360, 3D printing, Python, microcontrollers, data analytics and a hackathon) so students with no prior experience can join the project.
